How it works

Live in your AI chat in three steps

STEP 01

Connect with one link

Add the server to your AI client with a single connection link — no install, no config files, no infrastructure to run.

STEP 02

Type, speak, or snap

Type “spent $12 on lunch,” say it as a voice command, or snap a receipt or invoice. The AI reads it and records the amount, category, and date for you.

STEP 03

Ask for answers

“How much on food this month?” Get instant summaries, search past spending, and track budgets — all inside your normal chat.
